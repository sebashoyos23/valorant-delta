**The order matters**

1. Close the game **and** its launcher.
2. Run the loader as administrator.
3. Open **Spoofer** and press **Spoof**.
4. **Reboot Windows.** Volume serial and SMBIOS are read at boot — without the

account is not linked to the old ban.

Aimbot, ESP and skins are included and work normally once the machine is clean —
they are just not the point of this build.
